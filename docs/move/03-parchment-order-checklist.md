# 03 — Parchment Order Checklist (ITT sealed transcripts)

**Goal:** Official sealed transcripts / diploma verification for China Work Permit & Z visa.  
**You already hold** the physical diploma — employers and Chinese authorities still typically need **official sealed transcripts** (and often authenticated/apostilled copies).

---

1. **ITT transcript how-to (Parchment help):**  
   https://parchment.my.site.com/s/article/ITT-Technical-Institute-transcript-requests

2. **Parchment registration / order portals (ITT-related):**  
   - https://www.parchment.com/u/registration/19405892/account  
   - https://www.parchment.com/u/registration/26634911/account  

Use the article first to confirm which portal applies to your campus/records year, then create/order through Parchment yourself (free account on their site; transcript fees are Parchment’s published fees — this pack does not charge you anything).

---

## Before you start

- [ ] Full legal name exactly as on diploma / student records  
- [ ] Approximate attendance / graduation years (ITT Nashville)  
- [ ] Degree title: **Digital Entertainment and Game Design** (bachelor’s)  
- [ ] Shipping address that can receive sealed mail (or electronic delivery if offered and accepted by your future employer)  
- [ ] Confirm with future school HR whether they need: sealed PDF, mailed sealed envelope, or both  

---

## Step-by-step

1. [ ] Open the ITT Parchment help article and read current instructions.  
2. [ ] Open the correct Parchment registration/order link for ITT.  
3. [ ] Create / sign in to **your own** Parchment account.  
4. [ ] Search for **ITT Technical Institute** (Nashville campus / records).  
5. [ ] Request **official transcript** (sealed).  
6. [ ] If offered: request **diploma verification** or certified diploma copy — physical diploma alone is usually not enough for Z / Work Permit.  
7. [ ] Choose delivery:  
   - Prefer a method your Chinese employer accepts (often original sealed envelope or official e-delivery they can verify).  
8. [ ] Save: order confirmation #, PDF receipts, tracking.  
9. [ ] When received: **do not open** a sealed envelope if the school requires it unopened.  
10. [ ] Make a clear scan/photo of anything you *are* allowed to open/copy for your own files.  
11. [ ] Proceed to notarization + **TN apostille** (see `04-tn-apostille-packet.md`).
