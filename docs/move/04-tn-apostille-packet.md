# 04 — Tennessee Apostille Packet Prep

**Authority:** Tennessee Secretary of State  
**Guide:** https://sos.tn.gov/businesses/guides/apostille-authentication-guide  

You are a **Tennessee resident** → Chinese Embassy **Washington, DC** jurisdiction for visa; apostilles for TN-notarized documents go through **TN SOS**.

---

## What an apostille is (plain English)

An apostille is a state certificate authenticating a notary (or certain public officials) so a foreign country (China) can accept the document. China is part of the Apostille Convention (as of late 2023) — confirm current acceptance with your employer / embassy notices.

---

## Typical docs you will apostille for Z / Work Permit

| Document | Prep | Apostille via |
|----------|------|---------------|
| Diploma (notarized copy / affidavit) | Notary first | TN SOS |
| Official transcript (or notarized copy per SOS rules) | Follow SOS + notary rules | TN SOS |
| FBI Identity History Summary | Special case — see below | Confirm: often **US Department of State** for federal docs, not TN SOS |

**Critical:** FBI checks are **federal** documents. Do **not** assume TN SOS can apostille the FBI summary. Check current US Dept. of State authentications guidance after you receive the FBI result. Degree docs notarized in Tennessee → TN SOS apostille.

## What to notarize (typical pattern)

Exact requirements change — verify on the SOS page. Common pattern:

- [ ] Copy of diploma + notary certificate / affidavit that copy is true  
- [ ] Transcript-related affidavit if originals cannot be altered  
- [ ] Your signature on SOS request forms if any  

Notary must be **Tennessee** notary for TN SOS apostille path.
