Common questions — have answers

5–10 min demo structure (CELTA-style)

  1. Warmer (1 min) — greet, easy question, energy
  2. Context (1–2) — picture/story that needs the target language
  3. Clarify (2) — meaning, form, pronunciation of 4–6 items
  4. Practice (3–4) — controlled → freer (pair question, mini role-play)
  5. Feedback (1) — correct 1–2 errors kindly + close
Ask the recruiter: young learners or adults? Match materials to that.
