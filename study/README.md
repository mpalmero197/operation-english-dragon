# CELTA Self-Study Pack for Michael Palmero

**Warm welcome.** This pack prepares you for the *knowledge and skills* side of CELTA — language awareness, skills teaching, planning, classroom management, and the four written assignment genres — so you walk into an authorised course ready to learn fast.

> **Chosen pacing: 8 weeks steady.** About **1–1.5 hours per day**, six study days + a **weekend checkpoint quiz**. This is a marathon, not a cram: Week 1 materials are ready to use today ([lesson 01](lessons/01-learners-and-context.md) → [lesson 02](lessons/02-grammar-for-teachers.md) → [quiz-week1](exams/quiz-week1.md)). Tick boxes in [schedule.md](schedule.md) as you go.

## What this pack is

- A free, original study path mapped to the public **July 2024 CELTA syllabus** topics
- Lessons, quizzes, practice assignments, and an 8-week schedule (~1–1.5 hours/day)
- Honest preparation for teaching practice (TP) *concepts* — not a substitute for assessed teaching
- A light **Delta roadmap** for after CELTA foundations

## What this pack is NOT

- **Not a CELTA certificate.** Cambridge awards CELTA only through authorised centres.
- **Not a sit-down exam you can pass alone.** CELTA requires:
  - enrolment on an authorised course (typically 120+ contact hours)
  - **6 hours** assessed teaching practice with real learners
  - **6 hours** observation of experienced teachers (some may be video)
  - **4 written assignments** (usually 750–1000 words each)
  - continuous assessment + external moderation
- **Not Cambridge copyrighted course materials.** Lessons and practice items here are original. Syllabus topic titles are paraphrased from the free public PDF for orientation only.

> **Honest note:** Use this pack *before* or *alongside* a real CELTA. When you are ready for the certificate, find an authorised centre via Cambridge English and complete the assessed course.

## Suggested free companions while you study

- **ELT Concourse** – grammar, phonology, methodology guides (excellent pre-CELTA depth)
- **British Council TeachingEnglish** – articles, lesson ideas, classroom management tips
- A learner’s dictionary (e.g. Cambridge Dictionary online) for meaning / form checks
- Phonemic chart (British Council or similar free chart) for Week 3

## Success criteria for *you*

By the end of Week 8 you should be able to:

1. Talk confidently about learners, motivation, and context  
2. Analyse simple grammar / lexis / phonology for teaching (meaning–form–phonology)  
3. Design basic receptive and productive skills stages  
4. Draft a CELTA-style lesson plan outline with aims, stages, interaction, timing  
5. Write draft practice versions of all four assignment genres  
6. Know exactly what a real CELTA still requires (TP + observation + centre)
