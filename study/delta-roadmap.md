## What is Delta?

**Delta** (Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages) is an advanced teaching qualification, typically for teachers with experience after initial training (e.g. CELTA) and real classroom hours. It has **three modules** that can be taken separately.

| Module | What it is | Independent prep? |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| **Module 1** | Written examination — understanding language, methodology, resources | **Yes — heavily self-studyable** with official samples |
| **Module 2** | Developing practice — coursework teaching & reflection with an approved centre | Needs centre / supervised practice |
| **Module 3** | Extended assignment — specialism or ELT management option | Often with centre support; research-based |

You can complete modules in any order; many teachers start with Module 1 while teaching.

---

## Module 1 in brief

- Two papers (each 90 minutes, with a break)  
- Tests depth of language analysis, skills, methodology, resources, and exam technique  
- Grades typically Distinction / Merit / Pass / Fail  

**This pack’s CELTA Topic 2–5 work is a foundation**, but Delta expects greater depth, wider reading, and faster, denser exam answers.

## Suggested path AFTER CELTA foundations

1. **Teach** — gain classroom hours; keep a reflective journal (feeds Module 2 later).  
2. **Deepen language awareness** — advanced grammar/phonology/discourse; practise timed analyses.  
3. **Read methodology** widely (approaches, SLA basics, assessment, materials).  
4. **Sit Module 1** when ready — use handbook + official samples under timed conditions.  
5. **Module 2** with an approved centre when you can commit to observed teaching & assignments.  
6. **Module 3** — choose a specialism that matches your career (e.g. young learners, ESP, exam classes) or ELTM if managing.

## Honesty box

Delta Module 1 can be prepared independently, but Modules 2 (and typically 3) involve approved providers and serious time. CELTA remains the usual first step — finish an authorised CELTA before treating Delta as the main goal.
