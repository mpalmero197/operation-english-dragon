# Week 4 Checkpoint Quiz — Receptive Skills

**Lesson:** [05-receptive-skills](../lessons/05-receptive-skills.md) · **Qs:** 15 · Then attempt [midterm](midterm.md)

---

## Questions

**1.** Skimming vs scanning — one difference.  
**2.** Order: detail task, lead-in, gist, follow-up, pre-teach.  
**3.** What is blocking vocabulary?  
**4.** MCQ: Best first task for a long article: A. 15 detail questions B. gist match to summary C. write a parallel article D. conjugate all verbs  
**5.** Name two listening barriers + supports.  
**6.** Top-down processing — brief definition.  
**7.** Write one gist + one scan question for a train timetable text.  
**8.** Why play a listening twice (often)?  
**9.** Inferring meaning — example task type.  
**10.** Authentic vs graded text — one teaching implication.  
**11.** Follow-up after reading should usually…?  
**12.** Subskill label: learners highlight all dates in a biography.  
**13.** Schema — why activate it?  
**14.** For Assignment 3, you must relate task design to…?  
**15.** Critique: Teacher starts with “Explain paragraph 4 line 3 vocabulary in your L1” before any gist — issue?

---

## Answer key

**1.** Skimming = gist; scanning = specific info.  
**2.** Lead-in → pre-teach → gist → detail → follow-up.  
**3.** Words that would stop learners completing the task.  
**4.** B  
**5.** Speed→replay; unknown lexis→pre-teach; noise→headphones; accents→prediction (any two pairs).  
**6.** Using background knowledge/expectations to understand.  
**7.** Gist: Is this arrivals or departures? Scan: What time is the last train to X?  
**8.** Processing load; second chance for detail after gist.  
**9.** Attitude questions; “what does *this* refer to?”  
**10.** Authentic may need more scaffolding/adaptation.  
**11.** Personalise/use content productively (speak/write).  
**12.** Scanning.  
**13.** Improves prediction and comprehension.  
**14.** Skills/subskills development (with rationale).  
**15.** No schema/gist first; detail/vocab before global understanding.
