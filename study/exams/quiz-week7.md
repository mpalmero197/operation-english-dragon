## Answer key

**1.** Learners say yes without proof; doesn’t diagnose concepts/instructions.  
**2.** Writing or speaking? Alone or together? Compare with whom?  
**3.** Am I hungry for a meal now? *(Probably no)* Did I eat before now? *(Yes)*  
**4.** ICQ checks task instructions; CCQ checks language meaning.  
**5.** Delayed/selective after task unless message fails.  
**6.** Recast, prompt, delayed board, peer, gesture…  
**7.** Slip = performance slip; error = knowledge gap.  
**8.** *Look at the questions. Speak with your partner.*  
**9.** Task success; target language; problems.  
**10.** Everyone sees the model; papers don’t distract.  
**11.** Reformulate correctly: S: *He go* T: *He goes to work at 8.*  
**12.** Do I give you the pen temporarily? *(Yes)* Do you keep my pen forever? *(No)*  
**13.** Poor: more busywork unrelated; Good: extension on-aim / swap partners.  
**14.** Use names; eye contact; fair turns; smile; listen.  
**15.** Focused noticing + practice; not only one recast.  
**16.** Word order / missing endings / syllable issues.  
**17.** Breakouts confuse → ICQs + demo + written steps in chat.  
**18.** Stop; clarify with demo/ICQs; restart; don’t blame.
