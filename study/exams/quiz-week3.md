# Week 3 Checkpoint Quiz — Lexis & Phonology

**Lessons:** [03](../lessons/03-lexis.md), [04](../lessons/04-phonology.md) · **Qs:** 18 · **Pace:** weekend after Week 3

---

## Questions

**1.** Define collocation with an original example.  
**2.** MCQ: *look after a child* is best described as: A. tense B. multi-word verb C. passive D. schwa  
**3.** Analyse *do homework*: problem learners might say; correct collocation partner verb.  
**4.** Register: order from informal → formal: *children / kids / offspring* (approximate).  
**5.** What is a lexical set? Give a 4-item café set.  
**6.** Mark primary stress: *photography*, *photographic*, *photograph*.  
**7.** What is schwa? Where might it appear in *teacher*?  
**8.** Weak form likely for *can* in *I can go*?  
**9.** Feature name: *an ice* cream linking to /əˈnaɪs/? (linking across article+noun)  
**10.** Minimal pair purpose in teaching?  
**11.** Transcribe or describe vowels: *ship* vs *sheep*.  
**12.** Two CCQs for *out of order* (machine not working).  
**13.** Why teach word stress for *import* (noun vs verb)?  
**14.** Connected speech: name elision OR assimilation with a simple example.  
**15.** Lexis MFP: one phonology note for *comfortable*.  
**16.** Short answer: intelligibility vs “perfect native accent” as a teaching goal.  
**17.** Design a 3-step drill sequence for /θ/ in *think*.  
**18.** Mini LRT: *make a decision* — meaning, form, problem, 1 CCQ.

---

## Answer key

**1.** Words that commonly go together, e.g. *catch a bus*.  
**2.** B  
**3.** *make homework* → *do homework*.  
**4.** kids → children → offspring.  
**5.** Related topic words; e.g. menu, bill, tip, waiter.  
**6.** phoTOgraphy; photoGRAPHic; PHOtograph (standard patterns).  
**7.** Weak /ə/; second syllable of *teacher* /ˈtiːtʃə/.  
**8.** /kən/  
**9.** Linking (C-V).  
**10.** Contrast one sound difference to improve perception/production.  
**11.** /ɪ/ vs /iː/.  
**12.** Does it work? *(No)* Can I use it now? *(No)*.  
**13.** Stress shift changes word class/meaning perception.  
**14.** e.g. elision *next please*; assimilation *good boy*.  
**15.** Stress on first syllable; reduced middle syllables /ˈkʌmftəbl/ (variants OK).  
**16.** Aim for clear communication; native-identical accent not required.  
**17.** Example: isolate /θ/ → *think/ thank* → sentence drill.  
**18.** Decide something; *make + a + decision*; *do a decision*; Are you deciding? *(Yes)*.
