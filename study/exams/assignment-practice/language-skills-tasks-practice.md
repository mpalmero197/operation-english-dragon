## Text (original, authentic-style)

> **Weekend Market on River Street**  
> River Street Market returns this Sunday from 9:00 to 14:00. Over forty stalls will sell fresh fruit, bread, second-hand books, and handmade soap. Live folk music starts at 11:30 near the fountain. Families are welcome; please keep dogs on leads. Free bicycle parking is available behind the library. If it rains heavily, the market moves indoors to the Community Hall on Park Road — check the market’s social media page after 7:00 on Sunday morning for updates. Volunteers are still needed to help with litter collection between 14:00 and 15:00; email shifts@riverstreetmarket.example if you can help.

**Assumed level:** B1 adult general English.

## Your tasks

1. Identify **receptive** skills/subskills you could practise with this text (list with brief definitions).  
2. Design:  
   - Lead-in idea  
   - Gist task  
   - Specific information / detail task  
   - Optional language-from-text focus (one lexical set or grammar noticing)  
3. Identify **productive** follow-up (speaking or writing) with brief rationale.  
4. State any **barriers** and supports.  
5. Reference one methodology idea from a free source (TeachingEnglish / ELT Concourse) in your rationale.  
6. Simulate 750–1000 words or a tight 500-word version with headings.

## Sample task sketches (answer key style)

- **Gist:** Is this mainly advertising a market or complaining about one? / Choose the best summary.  
- **Scan:** What time does music start? Where is bike parking? Rain plan?  
- **Detail:** What should dog owners do? How can volunteers help?  
- **Follow-up:** Write a short social post inviting a friend; or pair planning a visit timetable.  
- **Barriers:** Dense info → table to complete; place names → map sketch.  
- **Rationale:** Gist before detail reduces overload; follow-up personalises content for memory.
