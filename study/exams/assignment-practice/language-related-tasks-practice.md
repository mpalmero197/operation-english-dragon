## Answer key (concise exemplars — expand in your write-up)

**1.** Meaning: report complete before Friday. Form: *will have + PP*. Phonology: *we’ll have* weak. Problem: confuse with *will finish*; CCQs: Finished before Friday? Still working Friday night?  

**2.** Meaning: strong advice. Form: *ought to + bare infinitive*. Problem: *ought to* + *-ing*; rare in speech vs *should*. CCQs: Good idea? Obligation as strong as *must*?  

**3.** Meaning: no coffee left. Form: *run out of + noun*. Problem: *run out from*; separable errors. CCQs: Is there any left? *(No)*  

**4.** Meaning: curiosity/willingness about applying. Form: *interested in + gerund/noun*. Problem: *interested to apply* (sometimes heard; teach pattern). CCQs: Adjective + preposition? Verb *-ing* after *in*?  

**5.** Meaning: if it does **not** rain… Form: *Unless + present, will + bare*. Problem: double negative *unless it doesn’t*. CCQs: Do we eat outside if rainy? *(No)*  

**6.** Meaning: design in progress now; focus on timetable. Form: *is/are being + PP*. Problem: omit *being*. CCQs: Finished already? *(No)* Focus on who? *(Not said)*  

References: learner dictionary for examples; teacher grammar for passive progressive notes.
