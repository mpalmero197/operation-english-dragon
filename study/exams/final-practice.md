### Part 2 (sample points)
**A:** Unreal advice; *If I were you, I’d + bare infinitive*; stress on *were/you*; problems: *If I was* variation, *will* instead of *’d*; CCQs: Am I giving advice? Imaginary situation?  
**B:** Activity from March until now; *has been + V-ing + since + point*; /sɪns/; *since* vs *for*; still true now? Started in March?  
**C:** Postpone; *put off + noun/gerund*; separable; confuse *put on*; CCQ: Later or now?  
**D:** Focus on handouts; *were + PP*; *-ed* pronunciation; agent omitted OK; CCQ: Do we say who printed?
