## Answer key

**1.** B — CELTA is not a single sit-down exam replacing TP; it requires assessed teaching practice and course components.  
**2.** B  
**3.** B  
**4.** B  
**5.** C  
**6.** B  
**7.** C  
**8.** B  

**9.** Any three: L1 backgrounds; levels; motivations/goals; preferences; age range; literacy; constraints (shifts, online); special needs; group size; previous learning experience.  

**10.** Any sensible: clear boardwork; written models of key words; allow dictionary photos; peer support; extra sound–spelling focus; avoid oral-only instructions.  

**11.** Learners meet both in real life; choose one productive model for consistency while teaching receptive awareness and intelligibility; respect goals/context.  

**12.** Example CCQs: Do I have my wallet now? *(No)* Did it happen in the past with a result now? *(Yes)* Do I say exactly when I lost it? *(Not required)*. (Accept equivalent meaning-focused CCQs; reject only “Do you understand?”)  

**13.** Example: Informer during clarification; facilitator during pair practice; monitor during task; feedback provider in delayed correction. (Any two with plausible timing.)  

**14.** Meaning: temporary situation around now / this week — not necessarily her permanent habit. Form: *is/am/are + verb-ing*. Problem examples: confusing with present simple habits; overusing continuous with stative verbs; missing auxiliary.  

**15.** After *can*, use bare infinitive (no *to*): *He can drive.*  

**16.** Transfer B; Rapport C; Needs analysis A; Guided discovery D.  

**17.** Examples: shorter speaking bursts; planning time before speaking; mingle with movement; change groupings; provide phrase support on board; start with a low-stakes warmer; switch one discussion to a structured information-gap. (Any two sensible.)  

**18.** Sample: The pack builds knowledge of syllabus topics, language analysis, skills teaching ideas, planning habits, and assignment genres. It cannot replace an authorised Cambridge centre course, six hours of assessed teaching practice, required observation, tutor feedback, or the official certificate.
