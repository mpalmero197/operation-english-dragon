# CELTA Study Syllabus Map

Mapped to the public **July 2024 CELTA Syllabus and assessment guidelines**  
Source (free PDF): https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/images/21816-celta-syllabus.pdf

**Pace:** 8 weeks steady (~1–1.5 hrs/day + weekend checkpoint). See [schedule.md](schedule.md) and [README.md](README.md).

> Reminder: this map prepares knowledge and skills. The certificate still requires an authorised Cambridge centre, 6 hours assessed TP, 6 hours observation, and 4 written assignments.

## The five syllabus topics

### Topic 1 — Learners and teachers, and the teaching and learning context
| Ref (public) | Focus |
|--------------|--------|
| 1.1 | Cultural, linguistic and educational backgrounds |
| 1.2 | Motivations for learning English as an adult |
| 1.3 | Learning and teaching preferences; teacher roles |
| 1.4 | Context for learning/teaching (physical or online); learner needs including difficulties/disabilities |
| 1.5 | Varieties of English; informed choices about models |
| 1.6 | Multilingualism and the role of first languages |

## Assessment components (what a real CELTA assesses)

### Component 1 — Planning and teaching
- **6 hours** assessed teaching practice with adult learners (typically ≥2 levels; at least some below intermediate)
- Lesson plans + delivery judged against public criteria categories, including:

| Category | Examples of what tutors look for |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Planning** | Clear aims; logical staging; timing; materials; interaction patterns; language analysis (M/F/P); anticipated problems + solutions |
| **Language focus** | Accurate models; clarifying meaning/form/phonology; appropriate practice; style/register awareness |
| **Skills** | Helping learners understand R/L texts; produce spoken/written language; develop writing |
| **Classroom management** | Rapport; instructions; grouping; monitoring; pace; questioning; feedback on tasks |
| **Reflection** | Noting strengths/weaknesses; responding to feedback; improving future plans |

### Component 2 — Four written assignments (≈750–1000 words each)
| Assignment | Typical focus | Pack practice |
|------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Focus on the learner** | Backgrounds, needs, preferences; language/skills needs; suggested activities + rationale | [focus-on-the-learner-brief](exams/assignment-practice/focus-on-the-learner-brief.md) |
| **Language related tasks** | Analyse items for meaning, form, phonology, use; problems; CCQs; reference sources | [language-related-tasks-practice](exams/assignment-practice/language-related-tasks-practice.md) |
| **Language skills related tasks** | Skills/subskills from a text; task design + rationale | [language-skills-tasks-practice](exams/assignment-practice/language-skills-tasks-practice.md) |
| **Lessons from the classroom** | Strengths/development needs; observations; action points beyond the course | [lessons-from-classroom-template](exams/assignment-practice/lessons-from-classroom-template.md) |

Full assignment guidance: [10-written-assignments-guide](lessons/10-written-assignments-guide.md)

## 8-week calendar (steady pace)

**Daily target:** ~1–1.5 hours (concepts + practice + glossary).  
**Weekend:** checkpoint quiz (15–20 Qs); optional light assignment draft.

| Week | Theme | Daily study targets | Weekend checkpoint |
|------|--------|---------------------|--------------------|
| **1** | Learners & context | Day 1–2: Topic 1 overview + lesson 01. Day 3–4: motivation, preferences, varieties, L1. Day 5: start lesson 02 grammar foundations. Day 6: review glossaries. | [quiz-week1](exams/quiz-week1.md) |
| **2** | Grammar for teachers | Lesson 02 in depth: tense/aspect, modality, voice, noun/verb phrases; practice analyses. | [quiz-week2](exams/quiz-week2.md) |
| **3** | Lexis + phonology | Lessons 03–04: word formation, collocation; phonemes, stress, connected speech. | [quiz-week3](exams/quiz-week3.md) |
| **4** | Receptive skills | Lesson 05: reading + listening staging, subskills, barriers. Mid-week: review Topics 1–3. | [quiz-week4](exams/quiz-week4.md) + [midterm](exams/midterm.md) |
| **5** | Productive skills | Lesson 06: speaking functions/fluency; writing process stages. | [quiz-week5](exams/quiz-week5.md) |
| **6** | Planning & resources | Lesson 07: aims, staging (e.g. PPP/TBL/skills), materials adaptation. Draft one mini plan. | [quiz-week6](exams/quiz-week6.md) |
| **7** | Classroom craft | Lessons 08–09: grouping, instructions, ICQs; errors, correction, CCQs. | [quiz-week7](exams/quiz-week7.md) |
| **8** | Assignments & integration | Lesson 10 + all four practice briefs; revise weak areas; sit final mock. | [quiz-week8](exams/quiz-week8.md) + [final-practice](exams/final-practice.md) |
